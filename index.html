Meet the Italian Maple-Pepper Club — The Only Sandwich That Matters.

The "Why"

The Crunch:

It starts with an Italian Herb & Cheese loaf, toasted until the Parmesan crust is fragrant and golden. If your bread doesn't have a personality, it's not a sandwich.

The Sweet & Heat:

Honey ham meets maple-wood smoked bacon. We cut that sweetness with the sharp, creamy kick of pepper-jack cheese. It's a flavor profile that hits every note.

The Double-Drizzle:

We don't choose between mayo and ranch. We use both. It's the cooling finish that ties the smoky, spicy, and savory elements together into one masterpiece.

Most sandwiches are just fuel. This one is an experience. Once you've had the perfect crunch-to-chew ratio of the Italian Maple-Pepper Club, there is no going back to the deli counter.
